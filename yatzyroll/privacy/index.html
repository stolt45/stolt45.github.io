Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 28, 2026

The short version

Yatzy Roll runs entirely on your device. We don't collect, store, or sell any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking.

Data storage

The app does not store any user data. Game state is held in memory while you play and is not persisted, transmitted, or shared.

No tracking

Yatzy Roll does not use any analytics frameworks, advertising identifiers, or tracking technologies. We do not collect usage data of any kind.

No third-party services

The app does not communicate with any servers or third-party services. It works fully offline.

No account required

The app does not require or support user accounts. There is no sign-up, no login, and no cloud sync.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. If we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of the app after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
